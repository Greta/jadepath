# Jadepath branding

The open jade ring brings a little movement to Jadepath. Its jade, emerald, teal, mint, and pale lime colors carry through the app against a cool charcoal background.

## Logo files

- `src/lib/assets/jadepath-flowing.png` is the 630 × 630 transparent master.
- `src/lib/assets/jadepath-flowing.webp` is the lossless 144 × 144 header image.
- `src/lib/assets/jadepath-favicon.png` is the 64 × 64 browser icon.

The header pairs the symbol with a visible Jadepath wordmark. The link's accessible name is “Jadepath overview,” so the image itself has empty alternative text.

## Midnight Jade

The theme uses subtle gradients, small corners, gentle shadows, and a touch of frosted glass in the dialogs. Colors and surface styles live in `src/theme.css`. The lighter accents use dark text to keep buttons easy to read.
